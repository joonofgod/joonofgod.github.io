RhythmCast Privacy Policy

RhythmCast ("we" or "the Operator") values the privacy of everyone who uses the RhythmCast app ("the App") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. The App has no sign-up or login and we do not run any server. Information you enter or generate is stored only on your device, and we cannot collect or view it.

1. Purposes of Processing

The App processes information on your device only for the following purposes.

  • Nickname, gender, date of birth: displaying your profile, calculating biorhythms, producing results such as autonomic age, and setting the theme automatically
  • Measurement history: showing heart rate, HRV and condition results and their history
  • Profile photo (optional): showing your profile image in My Page
  • Camera frames: extracting the heartbeat signal during measurement
  • Advertising information: serving ads and measuring ad performance through Google AdMob

2. Information Processed

A. Information you enter, stored only on your device

  • Required: nickname, gender, date of birth
  • Generated automatically: measurement history (heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), condition results, etc.) and app settings
  • Optional: a profile photo you choose (a copy is kept in the App's storage)

B. Camera frames (face measurement: front camera; finger measurement: rear camera and flash)

The camera is used only while you are measuring. Frames are processed in memory right away to extract the average skin colour only. Video and photos are never saved or sent anywhere. For face measurement, face position is checked with Google ML Kit face detection, which runs on your device; face images are not sent off the device.

C. Information the advertising SDK may collect automatically

Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, iOS IDFA, etc.), IP address, device information, and app usage and ad interaction data

3. Retention Period

Information stored on your device remains there until you delete the App or run "Reset device data" in My Page. Camera frames exist only in memory during measurement and are discarded as soon as it ends. Information collected by the advertising SDK is retained according to Google's policies.

4. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties. If you choose to use the share feature, the result image is sent through your operating system's share sheet to the app or person you select. This happens only at your own choice.

5. Outsourcing and Third-Party SDKs

We do not outsource the processing of personal information. The App includes the following third-party SDKs.

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): serves banner and rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads, it may collect and process advertising identifiers, IP address and usage data under Google's policies, and this data may be transferred outside Korea (for example, to the United States). Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google ML Kit face detection (Google LLC): runs only on your device and does not send face images off the device.

6. Destruction

Deleting the App or running "Reset device data" in My Page erases all information stored on the device (profile, measurement history, settings and profile photo), and it cannot be recovered. We keep no separate copy.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You can view, edit and delete your information in the App at any time.

  • View and edit: edit your profile in My Page
  • Delete: "Reset device data" in My Page, or delete the App
  • Advertising ID: on Android go to [Settings > Google > Ads], on iOS go to [Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking] to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads.
  • Camera permission: you can withdraw it in your device settings at any time; measurement will then be unavailable.
  • Notification permission: used only for the 9 AM reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device and do not go through any external server. You can turn them off at any time in My Page ("9 AM reminder") or in your device settings.
  • Photo access: used only when you choose a profile photo yourself. The chosen photo is kept only as a copy in the App's storage and is never sent off the device.
  • Tracking permission (iOS): before tracking your activity across other apps and websites for advertising, the App asks for your permission. You can still use the App if you decline; you will see non-personalised ads.

8. Security Measures

  • All information is kept only in the App's private storage on your device and is never sent to a server.
  • Camera frames are processed in memory only and never saved.
  • The App requests only the permissions it needs (camera, notifications, and photo access when you choose a profile photo).

Please keep your device itself secure (for example, with a screen lock).

9. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children. Children under 14 need the consent of a legal guardian to use the App.
